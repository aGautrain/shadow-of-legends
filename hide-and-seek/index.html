      How to play
      • Round 1: Blue seeks and Red hides. Round 2: the other way round.
      • Hide phase: hiders leave their fountain and find a spot. Seekers wait in theirs.
      • Seek phase: a hider who stays in the seeking team's vision for s in a row is caught. Brush hides you unless a seeker or ward is in that same brush, and walls block sight.
      • Seekers have Stealth Wards each: press W to place one at the cursor.
      • The team that catches the larger share of hiders wins; on a tie, the faster team.
      • Right-click move · Drag pan · Wheel zoom · Y lock camera · Space center · On touch: tap to move.
